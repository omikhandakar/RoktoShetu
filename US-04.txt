User Story: US-04
Title: Emergency Blood Request Creation
Priority: High
Assigned Member: Khandakar Abu Noman Omi (24-57272-2)

Description:
As a requester, I want to submit an emergency request specifying blood group, bags needed, hospital location, required time, urgency level (Normal/Critical), and prescription attachment so that nearby donors can be alerted.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. Form captures required blood group, number of bags, location, and deadline.
2. Allows attaching a prescription photo.
3. Broadcasts push alerts to verified donors within the target area.
